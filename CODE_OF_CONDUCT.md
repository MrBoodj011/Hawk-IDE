# Code of Conduct

Hawk is built in a professional, respectful, and security-conscious community.

## Our standards

Be constructive, respect different experience levels, review ideas rather than people, and protect private or sensitive information. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and unauthorized security testing are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the project or its community.

This policy applies in the repository and in public spaces where someone is representing Hawk.
